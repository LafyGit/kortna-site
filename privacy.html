Privacy Policy

Kortna (كورتنا) · Last updated 16 September 2026

What we collect: nothing. Kortna is fully local: no account, no sign-in, no servers of our own. Your results, streak and settings stay on your device and only leave it when you choose to share via the iOS share sheet.

In-app purchases: if you buy a game pack for Majlis Kortna, payment is handled by the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; it receives an anonymous device identifier and transaction details (product, date, status), never your name or email. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Tracking and ads: none. No third-party analytics and no tracking permission request.

Question reports are stored on your device only. Children: suitable for all ages; no personal data is collected. Deleting your data: Account → Erase everything, or delete the app.
